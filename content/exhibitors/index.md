## Solution Showcase

HUNTCON 2027 dedicates a booth session on Day 1 (16:30–18:00) for solution providers to demonstrate their threat hunting capabilities. This is an opportunity for attendees to explore tools, ask questions, and engage directly with the teams building them.

This is not a sales floor — exhibitors are selected for their relevance to the threat hunting community and their ability to bring value to practitioners in the room.
