*Version 1 — last updated 7 October 2026*

This notice explains what personal data HUNTCON collects through this website, why, how long we keep it, and the rights you have under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

---

## Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is **Sébastien Damaye**, organizer of HUNTCON, 160 avenue des Martyrs, 38000 Grenoble, France.

For any question or request about your personal data, contact **[sebastien.damaye@se.com](mailto:sebastien.damaye@se.com)**.

---

## What data we collect

| Form | Data collected |
|------|----------------|
| **Call for Papers** | Company, first and last name and email of the primary speaker and (optionally) of a secondary speaker, talk format, presentation title, description and uploaded PDF, and a record of your consent (date, time and version of this notice) |
| **Registration** | First and last name, email, company, role(s) at the event and round table topics of interest, and a record of your consent |
| **Contact** | First and last name, email, subject and message, and a record of your consent |

We only collect what you enter in these forms. We do not buy or receive your data from third parties.

---

## Why we use your data

| Purpose | Legal basis (GDPR Art. 6) |
|---------|---------------------------|
| Reviewing Call for Papers submissions and contacting speakers about the outcome | Your consent, given on the submission form |
| Publishing the name, presentation title and description of **accepted** speakers on this website | Your consent, given on the submission form |
| Managing registrations and communicating practical information about the event | Your consent, given on the registration form |
| Answering messages sent through the contact form | Your consent, given on the contact form |

We will not use your data for any other purpose, and we never sell it.

We do not publish photos or biographies of speakers unless they explicitly provide them for that purpose. Talks are not recorded or streamed. Photography during the event is covered by our [Code of Conduct](/coc/).

---

## Who has access to your data

- **The organizer**, Sébastien Damaye.
- **The speaker selection board**, for Call for Papers submissions only.
- **Google**, which provides the online storage used for the forms (Google Sheets, Google Drive and Gmail).
- **Website visitors**, for the information published about accepted speakers only.

Your data is not shared with sponsors, partners, exhibitors or any other third party.

---

## Where your data is stored

Form submissions are stored in a Google account (Google Sheets, Google Drive and Gmail) used by the organizer. Google may process data outside the European Union, including in the United States. Google LLC is certified under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, which the European Commission recognizes as providing an adequate level of protection.

## How long we keep your data

| Data | Retention |
|------|-----------|
| Call for Papers submissions not selected | Deleted 2 months after the selection decision |
| Call for Papers submissions selected | Kept for 5 years after the event, for the conference archives |
| Registrations | Deleted 3 months after the event |
| Contact form messages | Deleted 3 months after the event |
| Records of consent | Kept as long as the related data, as proof that consent was given |

## Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to:

- **access** the personal data we hold about you;
- **correct** inaccurate data;
- **delete** your data;
- **withdraw your consent** at any time, without affecting what was done before. Withdrawing consent for a Call for Papers submission withdraws the submission;
- **object to** or **restrict** some processing;
- receive your data in a portable format.

To exercise these rights, email **[sebastien.damaye@se.com](mailto:sebastien.damaye@se.com)**. We will answer within one month.

If you believe your rights have not been respected, you can lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority, the **CNIL** ([www.cnil.fr](https://www.cnil.fr)).
