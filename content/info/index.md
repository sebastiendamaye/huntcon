## Venue

**HUNTCON 2027** takes place at **EDENN**, the new Schneider Electric headquarters.

**Address:**  
1884 Boulevard de La Défense  
92000 Nanterre, France

---

## How to Get There

### By Metro / RER

- **RER A** — Station *La Défense (Grande Arche)*  
  From central Paris, take RER A westbound. The venue is a short walk from the station.

- **Metro Line 1** — Station *La Défense (Grande Arche)*  
  Direct connection from central Paris (Châtelet, Champs-Élysées, etc.)

- **Tramway T2** — Stop *La Défense (Grande Arche)* or *Faubourg de l'Arche*

### By Car

From the A14 or A86 motorways, follow signs to *La Défense*. Paid parking is available at the CNIT and surrounding car parks.

### By Train

- **Paris Gare Saint-Lazare** → La Défense: ~10 minutes via Transilien Line L
- **Paris CDG Airport** → La Défense: RER B to Châtelet, then RER A (~50 minutes)
- **Paris Orly Airport** → La Défense: OrlyVal + RER B to Châtelet, then RER A (~60 minutes)

### By Plane

The closest international airports are:

- **Paris Charles de Gaulle (CDG)** — ~40 km
- **Paris Orly (ORY)** — ~30 km

---

## Accommodation

The La Défense business district offers numerous hotels within walking distance. We recommend booking early as March is a busy period for conferences.
