## General

**Is HUNTCON free?**
Yes. Attendance is completely free, but [registration](/registration/) is mandatory. The [solution showcase](/exhibitors/) also comes with no cost to exhibitors — however, they are expected to bring their own devices required for their demonstration.

**Can I attend only one of the two days?**
Yes, you can attend Day 1 only, Day 2 only, or both.

**How many attendees are expected?**
The event is designed for up to 100 participants.

**Is this a recurring event?**
This is the first edition. We intend to organize HUNTCON every year.

**Who is this conference for?**
Threat hunting practitioners, SOC/CSIRT team members, detection engineers, cybersecurity leaders, researchers, and anyone with a strong interest in threat hunting — whether you're building a program, scaling one, or looking for peers to exchange with.

## Language

**What language are the talks in?**
Presentations are a mix of English and French. English is strongly encouraged, but French talks are accepted since many attendees are French-speaking.

**What about the round tables?**
All round tables are held in English to accommodate participants from across Europe.

---

## Talks & Round Tables

**How long are the talks?**
Each talk is a 1-hour slot, including 10 minutes for Q&A.

**How do the round tables work?**
Each round table is a 45-minute session led by an animator with prepared questions. During registration, you select the topics you'd like to contribute to. For each topic, 5 candidates are chosen from the registrants to go on stage and share insights with the audience.

**Can I be both a speaker and a round table participant?**
Yes, these roles are cumulative.

**Will talks be recorded or shared?**
Conference sessions may be recorded (focusing on the speaker and slides). We will communicate in advance whether recordings will be published.

**Are round table discussions recorded?**
No. Round tables are held under the Chatham House Rule — content shared during the discussions must not be attributed to any individual or organization.

## Venue & Logistics

**Where is HUNTCON held?**
At EDENN, the Schneider Electric headquarters — 1884 Boulevard de La Défense, 92000 Nanterre, France. It is a short walk from La Défense (Grande Arche) station.

**Is food provided?**
Yes. A buffet lunch and coffee breaks are included on both days during the event. Evening dinners, however, are at the charge of each attendee.

**Is there WiFi?**
No public WiFi will be provided at the venue, but a decent 5G connection is available on site.

**Is there a dress code?**
No formal dress code. Business casual or whatever you're comfortable in.

**Should I bring a laptop?**
It's not required. Day 1 is presentation-focused, and Day 2 round tables are discussion-based. Bring one if you like to take notes.

## Registration & CFP

**How do I register?**
Use the [registration form](/registration/). You will receive a confirmation email.

**How do I submit a talk?**
Use the [Call for Papers](/cfp/) form. Submissions must include a PDF of your presentation.

**When is the CFP deadline?**
The deadline will be communicated on the website and via email to registered participants.

**When will I know if my talk is accepted?**
All submissions are reviewed by the conference board. You will be notified once the review is complete.

## Photography & Privacy

**Can I take photos at the event?**
Yes, but you must respect the photography policy described in our [Code of Conduct](/coc/). "No Photo" stickers are available at the registration desk for anyone who does not wish to appear in pictures.

**Will photos be published?**
Photos and videos may be used for internal and external communications. Individuals wearing "No Photo" stickers will be excluded from any published material.
